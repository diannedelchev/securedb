Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · JULY 9, 2026
SecureDB is self-hosted software. The organization operating this installation (the "Operator") controls the data processed here and acts as the data controller. This policy describes what the software itself collects and how it handles that data; the Operator may publish additional policies that apply to you.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information SecureDB processes when you use this installation, why it is processed, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to all users with an account on this instance.

2. Information we process

SecureDB processes the following categories of information, all of which are necessary to operate a governed database-access service:

  • Account information — your name, work email address, assigned roles and account status, created and managed by the Operator's administrators.
  • Authentication data — a bcrypt hash of your password and server-side session records. Session cookies contain only an opaque token; your password is never stored or logged in plain text.
  • Query activity — the SQL statements you run, the connection and database they targeted, execution outcome, timing and row counts. This history powers the Query History screen and the approval workflow.
  • Audit records — security-relevant events (sign-ins, queries, blocked statements, approvals, access grants, policy changes) together with your user identity, IP address and browser user agent.
  • Approval and access-request content — the SQL, justification text and decisions attached to approval requests and just-in-time access windows.

SecureDB also stores database connection credentials configured by administrators. These are encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest, decrypted only inside the server's connector layer, and never transmitted to any browser.

3. How the information is used

The information above is used solely to:

  • authenticate you and maintain your session;
  • enforce access policies, approvals and role-based permissions;
  • provide you with your own query history and saved queries;
  • maintain the tamper-evident audit trail the service exists to provide; and
  • operate, troubleshoot and secure the service.

SecureDB does not use your information for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to operating the service.

4. Cookies

SecureDB sets a single first-party cookie, sdb_session, which holds an opaque session token. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite, is transmitted only over HTTPS in production, and expires after a period of inactivity as well as an absolute lifetime. No analytics, tracking or third-party cookies are used.

5. Third parties

SecureDB does not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. No analytics or telemetry is sent from the application. The web interface loads the IBM Plex fonts from Google Fonts; your browser's request for those font files is subject to Google's privacy policy. All other assets are served directly by this installation.

6. Data storage and security

All data is stored in the Operator's application database. Protections built into the software include AES-256-GCM encryption of stored credentials, bcrypt password hashing, hashed session tokens, CSRF protection on all state-changing requests, strict security headers, and parameterized access to the application database. Audit logs never contain passwords or decrypted credentials.

7. Data retention

Query history and audit records are retained for as long as the Operator's compliance obligations require — audit trails are a core function of the service and are generally kept for the duration of the Operator's audit retention period. Account information is retained while your account exists; deactivated accounts are preserved to keep historical audit records attributable. Session records are deleted when they expire or when you sign out.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because the Operator is the data controller for this installation, please direct such requests to your administrator or the Operator's privacy contact. Note that audit records may be exempt from deletion where retention is required by law or legitimate compliance interests.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be communicated by the Operator through its usual channels.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy on this installation should go to the Operator's administrators — typically the team that issued your account. Questions about the SecureDB software itself can be raised with the project maintainers.
